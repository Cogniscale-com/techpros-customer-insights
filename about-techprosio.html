About TechPros.io

TechPros.io offers a platform for senior business and technology professionals to participate in peer-group thought leadership which helps inform and shape future decision-making.

TechPros.io conducts dozens of 30 min C-level interviews each month which cover a range of industry and sector topics. Highlights from these and follow-on discussions with our sponsors inform our writers who produce content for eBooks, ‘market view reports’ and video magazines.

Participants benefit from peer group insights and new ideas to solve problems. They often meet at follow on events e.g. roundtables, fireside chats and panel discussions.

Sponsors benefit from market intelligence, brand awareness, new relationships and commercial opportunities.
